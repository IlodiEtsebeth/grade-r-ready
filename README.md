# Grade R Ready

Lovable prompt

Build a mobile-first web app for South African parents of Grade R learners only to track whether their child is ready for Grade 1 by December.

The app should be a simple parent dashboard, not a school management system. It should help parents complete a readiness checklist, follow weekly home activities from now until December, track progress, and download a final readiness report.

Target user

Parents or caregivers of Grade R learners.

Core goal

Help parents understand:

What their child can already do.

What still needs practice.

What activities to do at home each week.

Whether the child is becoming ready for Grade 1.

Main screens

Landing / welcome screen.

Parent login or sign-up.

Child profile setup.

Readiness checklist dashboard.

Weekly activities page.

Progress tracker page.

Final report page.

Dashboard requirements

Create a clean, modern, mobile-friendly dashboard with large buttons, simple cards, and easy navigation. The dashboard should show:

Child name and age.

Checklist completion status.

Weekly activity progress.

Skills grouped into categories.

A visible readiness summary such as “Needs support,” “Developing,” or “Ready.”

Checklist categories

Include these sections:

Social and emotional development.

Language and early literacy.

Early numeracy.

Motor skills.

Independence and routines.

Example checklist items

Use parent-friendly language like:

Follows 2-step instructions.

Recognises own name.

Counts objects up to 10.

Holds a pencil or crayon correctly.

Uses scissors.

Separates from parent with confidence.

Packs away toys.

Can wash hands and use the toilet independently.

Weekly activities

Include 12 weeks of simple home activities from now until December. Activities should be short and practical, around 10 to 15 minutes each, such as:

Reading a short story and answering questions.

Counting snacks or toys.

Rhyming games.

Tracing names and letters.

Cutting along lines.

Sorting objects by colour or size.

Pattern copying with blocks or beads.

Role-playing school routines.

Tracking and reporting

Allow parents to:

Mark checklist items as not started, developing, or mastered.

Add notes.

Upload optional photos of activities.

See progress over time.

Download a summary report at the end.

Design style

Make it:

Friendly.

Simple.

Trustworthy.

Bright but not childish.

Designed for parents, not teachers.

Mobile-first rules

The interface must work beautifully on mobile phones first, then tablet and desktop. Use responsive cards instead of wide tables, large tap targets, short forms, and clear action buttons.

Constraints

Only for Grade R learners.

Do not build school admin features.

Do not add complex teacher tools.

Keep onboarding fast and simple.

Keep the language clear and easy for parents to understand.

Success state

A parent should be able to sign up, add one Grade R child, complete the checklist, follow weekly activities, track progress, and download a readiness report.

Ask me any questions you need in order to fully understand what I want from this feature and how I envision it.
